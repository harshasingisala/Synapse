1. Spark Plug Check & Gap Adjustment

Remove your spark plug, clean off carbon deposits gently with a wire brush.

Check the gap (usually 0.6-0.7 mm for Gixxer). Adjust with a feeler gauge or small tool.

A clean and well-gapped plug = better combustion and mileage.

2. Throttle Cable Free Play

If your throttle feels sticky or too tight, adjust the free play to about 2-3 mm.

Smooth throttle = better control + fuel savings.

3. Fuel Filter Cleaning

If your bike has a small inline fuel filter, clean or replace it regularly.

A clogged filter reduces fuel flow and hurts mileage.

4. Ride at Steady Speeds

Cruise control for bikes is keeping steady throttle, around 40-60 km/h for city roads.

Avoid frequent sudden acceleration or braking.

5. Switch Off Headlight When Possible

If riding in daylight, and it’s safe/legal to do so, switch off the headlight to save battery and reduce load.

6. Keep Bike Clean and Lubricated

Dirt and grime increase friction on moving parts — clean chain, sprockets, and lubricate regularly.

Keeps mechanical losses down.

7. Use Original or Quality Spare Parts

Cheap spares break faster and cause engine inefficiencies.

Focus on spark plugs, air filters, and tires first.

8. Fuel Tank Rust Prevention

Use fuel stabilizers occasionally if you park for long periods.

Avoid water contamination (fuel cap gasket fix helps here).

9. Avoid Over-Revving at Start

Start gently, avoid revving the engine hard when cold.

This saves fuel and reduces wear.
